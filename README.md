# Welcome to the Super Awesome Expansion, Very Cool, Nice!
I am Edwin. Hi. This mod brings a wealth of new content to Melvor Idle, including dungeons, items, and spells. It is intended to branch off the existing lore within the game, so nothing should feel out of place. Additionally, this mod brings several balance changes to account for new content being added. These will be outlined in the sections below.

If you have any questions, feedback, or issues, this is the place to voice them. You can also reach me on the Melvor Idle Discord. Enjoy!

# New Content
## Attack Spells
### Zephyr Strike
Requirements: Level 54 Magic and 100 Hall of Wizards Clears
Runes: 3 Ancient Runes and 15 Air Runes
Max Hit: 70
Effects: 100% chance on hit to apply Frostburn
### Cold Snap
Requirements: Level 58 Magic and 100 Hall of Wizards Clears
Runes: 3 Ancient Runes and 15 Water Runes
Max Hit: 80
Effects: 100% chance on hit to apply Slow
### Avalanche
Requirements: Level 62 Magic and 100 Hall of Wizards Clears
Runes: 3 Ancient Runes and 15 Earth Runes
Max Hit: 90
Effects: 100% chance on hit to apply Bleed100
### Fierce Flames
Requirements: Level 66 Magic and 100 Hall of Wizards Clears
Runes: 3 Ancient Runes and 15 Fire Runes
Max Hit: 100
Effects: 100% chance on hit to apply Burn
### Wind Storm
